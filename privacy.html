Privacy Policy for Reminder Bot

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reminder Bot ("the Bot") handles information when used on Discord.

Information the Bot Uses

The Bot may use Discord identifiers that are necessary to provide its reminder functionality, including server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs. These identifiers are used only to determine where reminders should be sent and which Discord roles should be mentioned.

Message Content

The Bot sends preconfigured reminder messages. It does not intentionally collect, store, or analyze the contents of user messages unless such functionality is added in the future.

Data Storage

Reminder configuration data may be stored by the Bot operator in configuration files or other storage necessary to operate the Bot. This may include reminder text, scheduled times, server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs.

Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell personal information. Information used by the Bot is not shared with third parties except as necessary to operate the Bot through Discord and the hosting services used to run it.

Data Retention

Configuration information is retained only for as long as it is needed to operate the Bot. Configuration may be removed when a reminder is deleted, the Bot is removed from a server, or the Bot operator determines that the information is no longer needed.

Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may be hosted using third-party infrastructure. Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to independently collect personal information from children. Users must meet Discord's age requirements and comply with Discord's Terms of Service.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Bot's functionality or data-handling practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Bot operator through the Discord server in which the Bot is installed, or through any contact method published by the Bot operator.
